Buffer pH Quiz

Test your understanding with these five questions.

What happens to the pH of a buffer when a small amount of strong acid is added?

  1. What is the primary function of a buffer solution?

    Answer: To resist changes in pH when small amounts of acid or base are added.

  2. Which components make up an acidic buffer?

    Answer: A weak acid and its conjugate base.

  3. In the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation, what does the term log([A-]/[HA]) represent?

    Answer: The ratio of the concentration of the conjugate base to the weak acid, which influences the pH relative to the pKa.

  4. If the concentration of conjugate base equals the concentration of weak acid in a buffer, what is the pH?

    Answer: pH = pKa (since log(1) = 0).

  5. Why does a buffer's pH change very little when a strong acid is added?

    Answer: The added H+ ions react with the conjugate base of the buffer, forming the weak acid, thus minimizing the increase in H+ concentration.
